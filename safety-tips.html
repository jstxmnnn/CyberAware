Our Safety Tips

Use Strong Passwords

Create long, unique passwords and turn on two-factor authentication.

Avoid Phishing Links

Share less online and keep your ID, OTPs, and card details private.

Protect Personal Data

Don't click suspicious links or attachments. Check the sender first.

Browse Safely

Use secure sites and avoid public Wi-Fi for sensitive tasks.

Did you know?

Over 90% of cyberattacks start with a phishing email. One careful pause before clicking can stop them.
